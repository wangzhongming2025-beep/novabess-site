Under the Lid: PowerBox Engineering

What the spec sheet doesn't say — the build details crews and OEM buyers ask about.

Every PowerBox runs a 314 Ah LiFePO4 (LFP) pack watched by a multi-layer BMS — over-voltage, under-voltage, over-current, short-circuit, over-temperature and passive cell-balancing protection, with CAN / RS485 reporting for fleet and OEM telemetry. It charges three ways: AC wall input (a full ~5–7 h recharge on the 16 kWh unit), an MPPT solar input sized for panel arrays, and generator pass-through so a running genset tops it up while loads draw. The 1,500 W pure-sine inverter drives both tools and sensitive electronics. The sealed, air-cooled enclosure is rated for -25°C to +50°C operation at ≤75 dB(A) fan noise, and is rated ≥3,000 cycles at 80% DoD with low self-consumption so a stored unit holds charge between deployments. For white-label programs we expose the BMS protocol and can re-enclose or re-rate the pack to your market.

Frequently Asked

What is PowerBox used for in commercial and industrial settings?

PowerBox is the wheeled, industrial-grade portable power station NovaBESS builds for sites where fixed wiring is impractical: construction, telecom and cell towers, mining and exploration camps, emergency and disaster repair, remote O&M and field service, and temporary power for events or agriculture. It delivers 4.02 / 8.04 / 16.08 kWh from its own outlets — no electrician, no permit, no grid tie-in.

Can PowerBox replace a diesel generator on a work site?

For most temporary and sensitive loads, yes. It is silent (≤75 dB(A)), fume-free and indoor-safe, so it runs inside a site office, workshop or tent where a diesel generator cannot. It will not match a generator for days-long, whole-site prime power, but for tools, lighting, comms, pumps and backup it removes fuel, noise and exhaust entirely.

How long does a 16 kWh PowerBox run typical site tools?

The 16.08 kWh unit (1,500 W continuous) runs a mixed site load — LED site lighting, a drill/driver rotation, a laptop bank and a small pump — for a full shift or more, and recharges from a wall socket, solar array or generator. The 4.02 and 8.04 kWh units cover lighter or shorter duty. Because it charges from three sources, a multi-day job stays powered without a fuel run.

Can we private-label PowerBox under our own brand?

Yes. PowerBox is offered as a proven white-label model through our OEM / ODM program — your logo, label and packaging, with minimal engineering overhead for fast market entry. We also support custom variants (capacity, enclosure, BMS, protocol). See the OEM / ODM page for the 7-step cooperation process.

Is PowerBox safe to use indoors and on a work site?

Yes. It uses LiFePO4 cells, runs air-cooled at ≤75 dB(A), and operates from -25°C to +50°C. It carries CE, UN38.3, UL and IEC. One hard rule: a portable unit powers appliances through its own outlets only and must never be back-fed into a building's distribution panel — unsafe and, in most regions, illegal. For whole-site backup you need a fixed, properly commissioned system.
